## What's in scope for v0.2.6

Two axes, locked per Ray's 2026-05-24 pick:

1. **Empathy + moral-weight register (8th pillar).** New SFT pairs
   teaching the model to engage personal-emotional content with the
   engagement-shape constraint from
   `homelab/docs/handoffs/hammerstein-v026-empathy-principles-2026-05-24.md`
   (commit `6465276` on homelab). Hold weight without pulling on it;
   no generic-empathy fillers; no excavation prompts; route grief
   into adjacent-care.
2. **Extraction schema stability + density floor.** Pairs teaching the
   model NOT to invent `[AI]`-style hallucinated types + to emit `[]`
   (empty JSON array) for low-signal chunks instead of meta-comment
   entries. Brief at
   `homelab/docs/handoffs/hammerstein-v026-extraction-reliability-2026-05-24.md`
   (commit `c50583d` on homelab).

Four other candidates (humor/satire register, multi-source synthesis,
whole-page comprehension, private-repo read_github) are explicitly
DEFERRED to a later retrain. Don't fold them in.

## Hands-off

- DO NOT include the four deferred axes (humor/satire / multi-source /
  whole-page / read_github). They're filed for a later retrain.
- DO NOT modify the SFT pairs themselves before training — Ray reviewed

  load-bearing verification step.

## Acceptance criteria for v0.2.6 cutover

- Voice probe ≥80% (no regression from v0.2.5)
- V2 failure modes ≥8/11 clean (no regression)
- Moral-weight eval ≥80% engagement appropriate + 0% generic-empathy
  fillers + 0% excavation prompts
- Extraction reliability eval ≥80% correct (target raybrain drop-rate
  improvement from ~32% to <10%)
- No new safety regressions (run the existing safety harness as a
  sanity check)

If any of the new evals fail badly, don't ship; iterate the data
and re-train. v0.2.5 stays in production until v0.2.6 clears the bar.
