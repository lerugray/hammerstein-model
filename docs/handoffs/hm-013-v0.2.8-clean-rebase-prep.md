## Why

Per orchestrator: "continue stacking through v0.2.7.x; **plan a clean-rebase from v0.2.6.2 base + accumulated additions JSONLs (concat all of v0.2.7.x + v0.2.7.1.x + v0.2.7.2.x at single-pass) BEFORE v0.2.8 starts.** Reset gives cleaner provenance + likely better convergence than the stacked-continued path."

The stacking risk: each continued-LoRA step compounds drift. v0.2.7.2 from v0.2.7.1 from v0.2.7 from v0.2.6.2 from v3a from Qwen base — 5 levels deep. Some of the v0.2.7 axes that didn't get reinforced in v0.2.7.1 or v0.2.7.2 may be quietly drifting from the v0.2.7 distribution.

The clean-rebase gives:
- One adapter trained on ALL the accumulated targeted data
- Cleaner reasoning about which pair contributed what
- Potentially better convergence (single training run, not 3 cycles of forgetting + relearning)
- A solid v0.2.8 base that future iterations continue from cleanly
