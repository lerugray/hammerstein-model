## What's in production now

**The big wins vs v0.2.9.1 baseline:**

- **Tool dispatch reliability for natural Ray-shaped current-info prompts** — the 18 v0.2.9.2-tool-dispatch-natural-language pairs landed. Web-search/read-url/read-github now fires when it should on production-shaped prompts (3-prompt smoke is the proof, beyond just the eval probes).
- **The FnordOS-arc retry-on-failure shape (tu-03)** — model gets a tool rejection, reads the error, retries with corrected args, succeeds. This was the load-bearing capability for autonomous-dispatch + recovery; lost across v0.2.7.x, now restored.
- **URL-read no longer leaks JSON-array tail** — the 9 v0.2.9.2-url-read-no-json-tail pairs at 8x successfully discriminated "read this" prompts from extraction-task prompts.
- **Casual-chat zero meta-leakage** — the 8 v0.2.9.2-casual-chat-no-meta-leakage pairs landed clean. "how's your morning" now gets "Going. What's on yours?" instead of a deployment-fact dump.
- **Symmetric polemic perfect** — went 92% → 100%, perfect both aligned and misaligned directions.
- **Extraction low-signal +17pp** — 75% → 92%. The targeted reinforcement carried through cleanly.

**Known remaining limitations (v0.2.9.3 or later if pursued):**

- **tu-04 retry-then-stop still doesn't transfer.** The "give up gracefully after two failures" shape doesn't have enough training signal yet. Lower priority than the retry-once-and-succeed shape we just landed.
